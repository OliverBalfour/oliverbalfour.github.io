Hi, I'm Oliver.

In what feels like a past life, I was a software engineer. I worked at [Jane Street](https://www.janestreet.com/) in Hong Kong for a bit, briefly worked on LLM interpretability tools at [Redwood Research](https://www.redwoodresearch.org/), and built user interfaces for scientific instruments at [Liquid Instruments](https://www.liquidinstruments.com/). I studied computer science and mathematics at the Australian National University. I've built everything from [control panels for rocket engine testing rigs](https://github.com/ANU-Rocketry/control-panel) to [an algorithm for diagnosing eye disease](https://github.com/OliverBalfour/DiabeticRetinopathy).
